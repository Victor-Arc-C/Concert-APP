# Marketing landing (Showbound)

Scope: the public page at `/`: header, hero stage, cue sheet, journey, map, proof, join, footer. English and French.
Mode: Persuade. Audience: students and young fans who follow artists' tours. Action: join the waitlist; secondary: try the sample, sign in.
Constraints: no invented audience numbers, testimonials, partnerships or prices; acts and counts come from /api/gigs (live or labeled sample); keep the Impact site verification line; build path code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The promise said on a stage: "Never miss your favourite artists live." with real acts standing in the light. Refuses the centred hero over a phone mockup and the three-feature-cards row.
OWN-WORLD: Same world as the app: lavender haze stage, dark lattice truss, six moving heads, gel beams multiplied into the haze, acts with gel photos and pools of colour on the floor, ink buttons, Bricolage Grotesque 800 at display size, rounded section sheets, an ink join section with sweeping beams.
STORY: The visitor reads the promise, sees three real upcoming acts lit, understands how a far-away night comes together (ticket, travel, bed, total, each with its source), sees who plays where, and joins the waitlist.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: logo left, nav and ink "Join the waitlist" right with EN/FR; truss with six lamps across the top; the promise at 7.4vw left; one-line lead; email field plus join button; three acts on the floor at the bottom right in their pools. Mobile: same order stacked, acts three across under the form.
FORM: stage hero with floor acts (form #1, seed 09db1cc5, reroll 1).
SIGNATURE: lamps power on in turn and find the acts; beams lean to the pointer; pointing at or selecting an act spotlights it and dims the rest; joining swings every beam onto the confirmation. A follow spot walks the cue sheet when it scrolls in.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
